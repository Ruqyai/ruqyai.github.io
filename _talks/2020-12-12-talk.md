If you are interested in machine learning but don't know how to get started, all you need is a plan, a roadmap, and the courage to press on. I explain the big picture before you dive in, and the general steps of building a machine learning project.

I also gave this talk at GDG Wrocław, Data Science Week Guinea and DevFest UK & Ireland.
